# Code of Conduct

## Our Standards

We want our projects to be welcoming, respectful, and constructive spaces. Participants are expected to:

* Be respectful and considerate.
* Give and receive feedback professionally.
* Focus on what is best for the project and its users.
* Respect different viewpoints and experiences.

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, and disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository maintainer. Reports will be reviewed and handled as confidentially as possible.
