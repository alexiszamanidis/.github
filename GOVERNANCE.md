# Governance

These repositories are maintained by [Alexis Zamanidis](https://github.com/alexiszamanidis).

## Roles

* **Maintainer:** sole decision-maker for issues, pull requests, releases, and project direction.

There is no formal committee, voting process, or contributor ladder.

## Decisions

* Day-to-day changes and reviews are handled by the maintainer.
* Breaking changes and major releases are decided by the maintainer, taking community feedback into account when it is available.
* Security reports are handled privately as described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Contributions

Anyone is welcome to open issues and pull requests. Merging a contribution does not grant maintainer access or decision-making authority.

See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) for how to contribute.
